# Using Research Writer

Start by naming the skill and describing your idea in ordinary language. A rough interest is enough. You can supply sources, notes, a draft or an existing research record, but you do not need a finished research question.

Research Writer has **no default finished-work target or destination**. If either is missing, it asks what you want to produce and where it should go. If you have already specified both for the project, it should use those choices without asking again.

## Begin with an idea

> Use Research Writer to explore how public libraries support social connection. Help me develop a question-development brief here in chat. Map the territory, investigate a worthwhile niche, and compare possible contributions before we choose a direction.

The **territory** is the existing conversation: what people study, how they frame it and what the evidence establishes. The **niche** is a supported unresolved issue, tension or limitation. **Occupying the niche** means choosing a useful contribution and a feasible way to make it. Research can change the question. If close prior work already answers it, ask for a revised contribution rather than a stronger novelty claim.

## Choose the finished work

Adapt these prompts to your topic and audience. Replace the example destination when needed.

**Synthesis**

> Use Research Writer to write a 1,500-word synthesis of research on urban shade and pedestrian comfort for city planners. Deliver it here in chat. Compare findings, methods and conditions; explain disagreements and remaining uncertainties.

**Meta-analysis feasibility**

> Use Research Writer to assess whether these supplied studies support a quantitative meta-analysis of tutoring outcomes. Produce a feasibility memo here in chat. Check comparable outcomes, extractable effect sizes and uncertainty, study independence, and analysis capability before pooling. If the gate is not met, explain what is missing and provide a warranted alternative.

**Theory**

> Use Research Writer to develop a theory paper about how shared work routines affect coordination. Write a 2,000-word draft here in chat for organizational researchers. Separate established findings from proposed mechanisms, compare rival explanations, and identify observations that could challenge the theory.

**Experimental or design proposal**

> Use Research Writer to propose a feasible study comparing two public-sign designs. Create a Markdown proposal in the task workspace for a design research team. Specify the question, measures, comparison, practical constraints and analysis plan. Keep methods prospective and do not invent results.

**Humanities close reading**

> Use Research Writer to write an 800-word interpretive essay here in chat on how the supplied short story represents memory through its recurring objects. Use only the supplied text. Ground the argument in passages, consider a competing interpretation, and preserve ambiguity where the text warrants it.

**Hybrid work**

> Use Research Writer to synthesize research on neighborhood repair initiatives, develop an explanatory model, and propose a study that tests it. Create a new Google Doc for an interdisciplinary research audience. Distinguish the evidence review, original hypothesis and proposed investigation.

## Prepare an article for publication

> Use Research Writer to prepare my theory article for a peer-reviewed journal. Deliver a revised draft and preparation checklist here in chat. Help assess venue fit, align each aim with its supporting argument or evidence, and identify missing authorship, funding, ethics and availability information. Do not invent declarations or submit anything.

For empirical manuscripts, provide the actual methods and results. Missing results stay missing; proposed studies remain proposals. The skill treats journal requirements as venue-specific and checks current guidance when a venue is selected.

## Refresh and position before drafting

> Use Research Writer to resume this project. Before drafting, confirm the readers, paper type and intended journal type or no-journal status. Review my previous search record, renew the literature where its date or scope requires it, and explain what the new evidence changes. Compare the closest published paper, summarize our proposed increment in one sentence, and challenge duplication, significance and feasibility. Keep novelty provisional where access is incomplete. Deliver the resulting review here in chat.

**Tutorial**

> Use Research Writer to plan a tutorial on evidence synthesis for practitioners who know spreadsheets but not statistics. Deliver the plan here. Compare the closest existing explanations, identify a useful teaching contribution, and build a sequence of concepts, worked examples, pitfalls and exercises. Label toy data and do not invent research results. No journal is intended yet.

The skill reuses choices already supplied. It should not rerun an identical fresh search, browse outside a supplied-only boundary, or claim that a single sentence proves the gap. It may recommend a meaningful replication, a different contribution, or stopping a novelty claim already covered by prior work.

## Set boundaries and depth

Useful additions include the audience, length, relevant disciplines, date range, exclusions, source access and practical constraints. Specify “use only these materials” when outside research would be inappropriate. For exploratory work, state an effort limit or ask for an initial bounded search followed by the most consequential unresolved questions. More sources alone do not establish deeper research.

## Continue and deliver

For sustained work, request a task-local research record containing decisions, source support, searches, unresolved claims and the next action. To resume, supply that record and say what changed. The skill does not imply automatic research between turns.

External delivery depends on supported tools and access. Distinguish a new document from edits to an existing one; for existing documents, provide the target and edit scope. An authorized creation can proceed without a blanket approval step. If delivery fails, the draft should be preserved and the specific obstacle reported. See the [validation record](validation.md) for executed checks and remaining limitations.


## Use research and molecular tools

> Use Research Writer to organize these references and audit the evidence behind my draft. Deliver the report here. Normalize identifiers, retain conflicting/version metadata, distinguish identity from full-text support, and check numeric claims against the exact recorded condition and artifact.

> Use Research Writer to prepare a computational feasibility memo here for a protein/cofactor idea. Inspect my device profile, distinguish the roles of sequence models, folding, docking and simulation, compare compatible optional tools, and propose bounded controls. Do not download weights or run remote jobs yet.

> Use Research Writer to inspect this FASTA and compute descriptors plus a seeded RDKit conformer for this small molecule in my local environment. Save reports in the selected project folder, record versions and hashes, and explain what these calculations cannot establish.

Tool commands, input schemas and optional setup are in the [tool usage guide](../skills/research-writer/references/tool-usage.md). A source-audited catalog entry does not mean that engine is installed. Profiles are private user configuration, not shared defaults or authorization to spend money or transmit data.

For a selected existing backup machine, follow [verified SSH backups](../skills/research-writer/references/off-device-backups.md). Ask the skill: “Back up this checkpoint store to my existing SSH destination. Establish identity, data permission and size limits, verify readback and show a recovery drill.” No destination or transfer is assumed.

For larger selected checkpoint backups or a changed recovery client, follow [streaming and profile migration](../skills/research-writer/references/streaming-backups.md). Ask Research Writer to establish the destination and byte/chunk/time budgets, verify the existing backup through the new profile, and create separate recovery state.
